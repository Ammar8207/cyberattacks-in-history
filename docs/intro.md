# Cyberattacks in History: Introduction

## Welcome

This book traces pivotal cyber incidents from the 1980s to the modern day—explaining how attacks evolved, why they mattered, and what we can learn to build resilient systems.

## What This Book Covers

- Core concepts and attack types
- Early worms and email-based outbreaks
- State-sponsored operations and industrial sabotage
- Data breaches and privacy impacts
- Ransomware economics and double extortion
- Supply chain compromises and infrastructure attacks
- Lessons learned and future outlook

## Who This Book Is For

- Students of cybersecurity
- IT and security professionals
- Curious readers exploring digital history and cyber warfare

## How to Read

- Start here, then proceed through chapters in order
- Each chapter stands alone, but together they form a coherent narrative
- Use headings, callouts, and examples as guides—not just summaries

:::tip
For the best reading experience, use the sidebar to navigate chapters by era and topic.
:::
