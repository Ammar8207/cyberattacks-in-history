# About the Book

"Cyberattacks in History: Major Incidents That Shaped the Digital World" is a professionally curated, long-form documentation project that traces landmark cyber incidents across four decades.

## Purpose

- Provide clear, beginner-to-intermediate explanations of major attacks
- Offer historical context and practical lessons
- Help readers understand evolving threat actors, techniques, and defenses

## Audience

- Students of cybersecurity
- IT and security professionals
- General readers interested in digital history and cyber warfare

## Start Reading

Begin with the [Introduction](/docs/intro), then follow the chapters in order from foundations to modern supply chain attacks.
